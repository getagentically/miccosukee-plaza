About the Plaza

Built for the road. Rooted in the Everglades.

Miccosukee Plaza opened on September 1, 2026 at 36340 SW 8th Street, right on the Tamiami Trail in western Miami-Dade County, between the Miccosukee Casino & Resort and the Miccosukee Indian Village.

It's a brand-new, state-of-the-art plaza for anyone traveling the Trail: a gas station, a food court, a general store and gift shop, and educational exhibits about the Everglades and the people who have called it home for generations.

Miccosukee Tribe of Indians of Florida

Stewards of the Everglades.

The Miccosukee Tribe of Indians of Florida has lived in the Everglades for generations and was federally recognized in 1962. The Tribe's constitution charges it with protecting the natural resources of its homeland, and today it co-stewards Everglades National Park alongside federal partners.

Along the Tamiami Trail the Tribe also welcomes visitors at the Miccosukee Indian Village & Museum and the Miccosukee Casino & Resort. The Plaza is the newest way to stop, learn, and support the Tribe's presence in the Everglades.
